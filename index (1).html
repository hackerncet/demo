About Meridian & Stone

A studio of architects, engineers and craftsmen united by one obsession — buildings that outlast trend and outperform expectation.

Founded in 1998, Meridian & Stone has grown from a boutique residential practice into a full-service architecture and construction house, delivering private estates, hospitality landmarks and commercial towers across three continents. Every project is led end-to-end by the same team — from first sketch to final walkthrough — so nothing is lost in translation.

Our Process

Five phases. One continuous team.

A transparent, disciplined build process — from concept sketch to the day you turn the key.

Phase 01 — Discovery

Site & Brief

We study the land, the light and your ambitions before a single line is drawn, establishing budget, timeline and vision.

Phase 02 — Design

Concept & Schematics

Our architects develop concept massing, materiality and floor plans in close collaboration with you.

Phase 03 — Engineering

Structural Documentation

Structural, MEP and permit documentation is finalized with full engineering rigor.

Phase 04 — Construction

Build & Site Management

Our own crews execute the build with weekly client reporting and on-site quality control.

Phase 05 — Handover

Finish & Key Handover

Final walkthrough, snagging and a full documentation package delivered at handover.
